Origin

Who is Spider-Man?

Peter Parker is a student from Queens who was bitten by a radioactive spider. That bite gave him the strength, speed, and senses of a spider — and a duty to protect New York City.

Behind the mask he is still a kid with homework, rent, and a sense of humor. On the rooftops he becomes the hero who swings between skyscrapers and stops crime before the city even knows it started.

What makes him different

  • Science nerd who builds his own web-shooters
  • Protects ordinary people, not just famous landmarks
  • Never gives up — even when the suit is torn

Abilities

Powers & toolkit

Classic spider abilities plus homemade gadgets. Here is a quick list of what he brings to a fight.

  • Super strength — lifts cars and punches above his weight
  • Wall-crawling — sticks to glass, brick, and steel
  • Spider-sense — danger warning before the hit lands
  • Web-shooters — swing, trap, and zip across the city

Story timeline

  1. Radioactive spider bite changes everything

  3. First nights as a masked crime-fighter in Queens
  4. Facing villains like Green Goblin and Doc Ock
  5. Becoming New York’s most trusted street-level hero
