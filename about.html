About the index

Behind the guide.

Which Spider-Man should you watch first? Spider Index gives each screen series its own starting point.

Four ways into the story

Start with Tobey Maguire’s Queens, Andrew Garfield’s Oscorp, Tom Holland’s high school or Miles Morales’s Brooklyn. The era guide introduces each world and its first film.

Explore the film archive for cast, directors and release order. Save titles as you browse, then open your watchlist when you are ready to choose a film.

The team

Ali and Arlan developed Spider Index as a web development project for group SE-2515 at Astana IT University. The guide is in English for fans discovering or revisiting Spider-Man on screen.

Sources and artwork

Film posters and stills come from Sony Pictures. The spotlight streams short trailer previews provided courtesy of iTunes. Its store badge opens the featured film on Apple’s store. The era guide retrieves film credits from Wikidata. Spider-Man and related characters belong to their respective rights holders.
