About the index

Behind the guide.

Pick a first issue, get to know the person behind the mask or choose a Spider-Man film for tonight.

Start on the page

The origin story follows Peter from Queens to his first appearance in print. Our reading guides give the series, year and issue numbers to look for, including starting points for Miles Morales and Gwen Stacy.

Find your screen series

Start with Tobey Maguire’s Queens, Andrew Garfield’s Oscorp, Tom Holland’s high school or Miles Morales’s Brooklyn. The era guide introduces each world and its first film.

Explore the film archive for cast, directors and release order. Save titles as you browse, then open your watchlist when you are ready to choose a film.

The team

Ali and Arlan developed Spider Index as a web development project for group SE-2515 at Astana IT University. The English-language guide is for new readers and film fans.

Sources and artwork

Issue covers and publication records come from Marvel Comics. Each reading guide links to its sources. Film artwork and featured trailers come from Sony Pictures, and the era guide retrieves film credits from Wikidata.
