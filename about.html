Student by day. Web-slinger by night. This page covers a short bio and the people behind this fan website.

Biography

Peter Parker

After the spider bite, Peter learned the hard way that power without responsibility can hurt the people he loves. He fights crime in New York while trying to keep his identity secret from friends, teachers, and villains.

  • Hometown: Queens, New York
